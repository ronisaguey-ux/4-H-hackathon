    Scientific Integrity

    Nutritional Mimics & Abiotic Stress

    Severe nitrogen deficiency causes V-shaped yellow chlorosis along the leaf midrib, and drought stress produces leaf rolling. CornShield AI isolates fungal necrotic lesions and hedges to the "unsure" path when abiotic chlorosis is predominant without characteristic fungal structures.

    Insect Frass vs. Tar Spot Stromata

    Insect feces (frass) closely mimic tar spot fungal stromata. The app's calibration engine explicitly prompts growers to execute the physical "Water Rub Test": if black specks wash off when rubbed with a moist rag, they are frass and require no chemical spray.

    Field Decision-Support, Not Regulatory PCR

    CornShield AI provides rapid in-field decision triage to time fungicide applications before canopy loss. It does not replace official USDA-APHIS or Land-Grant molecular PCR assays required for interstate quarantine or certified seed export.

    Smallholder Affordability & Economics
